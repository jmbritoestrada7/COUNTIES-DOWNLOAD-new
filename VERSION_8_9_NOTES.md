# VERSION 8.9 — Marketing date history, filters, and county-card controls

Changes:
- Marketing dates are larger and rendered as colored pills.
- Date colors are editable: 0–30, 31–90, 91–180, and 181+ days.
- Shows number of distinct sends in the selected/current year plus total contacts/items for that year.
- Marketing Map Filter can filter counties by channel (Mailer, RVM, AI Texting, Cold Calling, Neutral Postcard), year, from date, and to date.
- "Show only counties with matching marketing activity" hides all other counties without deleting/changing STR/property data.
- Optional county coloring by latest matching marketing date.
- County Card Display selector is now isolated in its own bordered box with a larger title.
- All new view/filter/color preferences autosave with the map.
- V8.8 safe marketing import / Render stability changes are preserved.
